A hidden activity beneath the soil.

  1. Eggs wait in the soilA protective mass carries them through winter.
  2. Juveniles hatchJ2 larvae set off to find a host root.
  3. They release ascarosidesascr#3 and ascr#18 spread through the soil, long before any symptom. This is what we read.
  4. One enters the rootIt settles, and a feeding site swells the root.
  5. The plant shows itYellowing and stunting appear only now, and new eggs return to the soil.
