# Integrated HP

01 - Loop

## What integration would require

The reviewed Human Practices evidence contains two dated consultation records, but it does not yet contain a verified feedback-to-change loop. The notes document concerns and suggestions; they do not show that the team accepted a recommendation, changed an artifact, tested the change, or returned to the participant.

A defensible loop needs a timestamped prior design state, the stakeholder question, the team's decision and rationale, a responsible owner, a changed implementation artifact, a relevant test, and follow-up that asks whether the response addressed the original concern. Without that chain, this page must remain a decision framework rather than a claim of integration.

02 - Cases

## Three open decision files

### The readout

An April interview note emphasizes real outputs and data, and raises practical questions about a freeze-dried yeast format. The reviewed files do not show that this advice changed the reporter, packaging, or operating workflow. To close this case, the team would need to record the original readout design, a decision, the resulting artifact, performance and usability tests, and follow-up on whether the evidence answers the practical concern.

### The target

A plant-science consultation note records uncertainty about analyte abundance, taxonomic association, specificity, and the decision value of routine monitoring. It also records an alternative use case as a possibility. No target change or validated specificity claim follows from that discussion. A completed case would require comparative evidence for the candidate target, a documented selection criterion, and experimental results that support or reject the decision.

### Deployment

The second consultation recommends controlled laboratory evidence before premature field ambition and raises implementation constraints. No field workflow, controlled-use format, regulatory path, or deployment test is documented as a consequence. The appropriate next gate is a bounded use scenario with biosafety, sampling, storage, operator, waste, and result-interpretation requirements defined before any field claim.

03 - Reflection

## Specific evidence before an integration claim

**Current documented status**

Consultation is documented, but causal project change is not. Negative advice and unresolved questions remain useful because they define evidence gates; they should not be converted into a success narrative. This page will support an integrated Human Practices claim only after at least one complete, auditable loop is present.
