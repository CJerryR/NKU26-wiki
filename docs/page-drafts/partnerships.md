# Partnerships

01 - Partners

## No sustained partnership is verified for publication

The reviewed materials contain invitation templates and sponsor-contact planning, but those records do not establish an accepted relationship, delivered support, or permission to identify an organization. A partnership invitation is a proposal, not a contract, receipt, role record, or logo permission.

Names and logos are therefore withheld. This does not imply that any organization declined support; it means the required evidence and publication permission were not located.

### Relationship record

Confirm both parties, the agreed scope, dates, responsibilities, duration, and approval to describe the relationship publicly.

### Delivery record

Confirm what support or output was actually delivered, when it was received, its value to the work, and the source evidence.

02 - Story

## No evidence-backed partnership timeline yet

Without confirmation of acceptance, sustained interaction, and delivered outcomes, the reviewed planning documents cannot support a public partnership story. A future account should distinguish proposed activities from completed work and record changes, limitations, and benefits without overstating either party's role.

**Future publication gate**

Require counterparty confirmation, exact scope, dated interaction records, delivered outputs or support, acknowledgement terms, and name or logo publication permission.
