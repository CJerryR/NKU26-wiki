## A signal chain we can test, step by step.

SUBSURFACE is an investigation rather than a device: literature-grounded ascaroside cues, receptor candidates, a yeast signalling chassis, and a reporter readout still to be chosen and tested.

1. cue**Nematode ascarosides**

Chemical trail in soil, treated as a literature-derived target input.

2. receptor**Heterologous GPCR candidate**

A candidate recognition layer; its ascr#18 response is untested and must be measured directly.

3. chassis**Engineered yeast pathway**

A synthetic-biology platform intended to couple receptor activation into a cellular signal, still under construction.

4. readout**Proposed reporter readout**

Unselected: the measurement layer where results and limits will be recorded.

Case file stays open

## Follow the biology below the surface.

The questions stay open: whether the candidate GPCR responds to ascr#18 at all, whether a rewired yeast pathway could carry that signal, and what a reporter would have to show to be trusted. Negative and inconclusive records are part of the trail.
