01 - Need

A possible field layer, not a finished device

The project is investigating a yeast-based sensing direction, but the reviewed records do not establish a field-device specification, a validated sampling workflow, or a measured readout requirement. Hardware may become useful only after the biological signal, sample preparation, operating conditions, and user needs are supported by evidence.

This hidden interim page therefore records missing evidence rather than presenting a conceptual enclosure as a built product.

03 - Build

No verified prototype or test result

No documented hardware prototype, assembly record, bill of materials, or performance test was located in the reviewed evidence. The page remains hidden so that proposal-stage ideas are not mistaken for a fabricated or validated device.

Future publication gate

Publish only after the team confirms requirements, versioned design files, component sources and costs, assembly instructions, safety review, test methods, raw measurements, limitations, and reproduction permission.
