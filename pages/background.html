03 - Status quo

Why the problem is not solved

Survives in soil

Eggs and cysts remain viable for years, defeating crop rotation alone.

Spreads invisibly

Movement of soil, water and equipment carries infestations between fields.

Few good tools

Resistant cultivars and nematicides are limited, costly, or environmentally fraught.
