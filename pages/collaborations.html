01 - Overview

No confirmed collaboration is ready to publish

The reviewed materials include outreach and contact preparation, but no evidence package confirming a completed collaboration with another team. The reviewed exchange records contained no confirmed collaboration entry.

Contact planning and invitation templates do not establish that another team accepted, participated, received value, or approved public identification. This page remains hidden until those points can be checked.

02 - Activities

The publication gate for two-way work

Counterparty confirmation

Confirm the other team, the contact record, the relevant dates, and permission to publish its name.

Shared activity evidence

Record what each side actually did, when it happened, and which source documents support the account.

Mutual outcome evidence

Confirm concrete outputs, how both sides used them, limitations, and mutual approval of the final description.

Evidence before a collaboration claim

A future entry must link the counterpart, dates, reciprocal work, deliverables, benefit to both sides, and publication approval. Until then, no team or outcome is named.
