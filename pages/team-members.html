01 - Members

What can be published now

The verified public identity is NKU iGEM 2026 at Nankai University. This is an interim evidence-status page: an authoritative complete member roster has not been located, and approved public display names, verified person-by-person roles, biographies, and public-image permissions have not been confirmed.

Member cards are absent because the roster, display names, and roles remain unverified. Photographs are also absent because publication permission was not verified; this does not mean that anyone refused permission.

Identity verified

NKU iGEM 2026 and Nankai University are supported by the reviewed records.

Roster pending

One authoritative roster must be confirmed before a complete directory can be published.

Names pending

Each approved public display name requires a separate confirmation record.

Roles pending

Broad workstream labels do not establish a person's role or contribution.

Biography optional

Any biography must be approved and may be omitted without affecting attribution.

Images withheld

No photograph is shown until its exact file, credit, caption, alt text, and publication permission are confirmed.

02 - Sub-teams

Broad workstreams, not a roster

The reviewed records organize work into three broad workstreams: Wet Lab, Dry Lab, and Human Practices. These workstreams do not establish person-by-person assignments or membership.

Wet Lab

Reviewed Wet Lab evidence consists of documented experimental records and attempted workflows. It does not show an end-to-end detector.

Dry Lab

Dry Lab evidence supports literature research and conceptual computational preparation. No validated model, software, or hardware system is documented.

Human Practices

Human Practices records include documented education materials and consultation records. They do not establish a completed integrated cycle.

03 - Affiliation

Identity and count boundary

The team identity is NKU iGEM 2026 at Nankai University. Reviewed internal materials use inconsistent counting conventions, so the numeric team count is withheld and identities are withheld until an authoritative roster and permission record are confirmed.

The reviewed partnership invitation is a proposal. It is not a roster, not a role matrix, not an identity permission record, and not a photo permission record. It therefore cannot establish membership, individual roles, image rights, sponsorship, or delivered support.

04 - Thanks

Final directory publication gate

A final directory entry will require an authoritative roster, an approved public display name, a verified role or contribution, and an optional biography. Any image entry must also identify the exact authorized photo file, caption and alt text, photographer or source credit, and publication permission.

The current person-by-work verification status is recorded in Attributions.
