# Homepage world-map case photos (v8.1)

Representative species images for the seven world-map case cards. None is a
field photograph from its marker location; every card says so on the front.
Files are 720 px WebP copies of the originals in `nematode_map_seven_images_v2`
(captions, credits, source and licence URLs are in `js/home-maps-data.js`,
`photos`). Brazil is cropped from Fig. 6 (Pb group) of Machado, Amaro & Silva
2019 (CC BY 4.0) — keep the word "cropped" in its credit.
