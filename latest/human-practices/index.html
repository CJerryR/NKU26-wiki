Starting from what is documented

An internal progress document contains two dated consultation records. Other inspected materials, including an interview brief and question lists, are preparation only and do not prove that additional interactions occurred. Public-attribution consent is unverified, so this page withholds names, identifying details, and direct quotations.

The notes are neither transcripts nor independently verified scientific or legal evidence. We treat every attributed point as a concern or suggestion recorded at the time, not as an accepted project conclusion. No completed feedback-to-implemented-change loop is documented in the reviewed files.

Two recorded consultations and the questions they opened

21 April 2026

The internal note records a discussion after class with a university plant-science instructor. It records concerns about access to relevant soil samples, storage and timing around experimental use, and whether a very low analyte abundance would leave substantial analytical error even after amplification.

Premise under review

The same note records uncertainty about taxonomic association and specificity, the actual agricultural decision value of routine monitoring, and whether an alternative target or use case should be considered. It also records possible biological and legal constraints on collecting or moving samples. These concerns are not verified facts or adopted conclusions.

27 April 2026

A second internal note records an interview with an alumnus who had iGEM experience. The suggestions included planning around long yeast experiment cycles and testing whether a Gpa1 modification is necessary rather than assuming it is required. Neither strategy is documented as adopted or validated.

Evidence sequencing

The interview note suggests combining computational candidate selection with wet-lab screening, keeping ODE work tied and subordinate to experimental data, and prioritizing controlled laboratory evidence before field claims. It also emphasizes real outputs and data for judging. These are recorded suggestions, not proof that a workflow changed.

Implementation questions

The note also raises freeze-dried yeast practicalities, including possible energy requirements for signaling, and recommends framing sponsorship or partner requests around technical value. No freeze-dried format, partnership model, or sponsorship strategy is documented as implemented because of this advice.

What this challenged

Together, the records expose open questions about analytical feasibility, specificity, actual decision value, experimental sequencing, evidence quality, controlled use versus field use, and implementation constraints. They guide what must be investigated; they do not establish that stakeholder input changed the project.

No verified change loop yet

  1. Evidence gate

    Trace the decision

    No specific implemented change is evidenced in the reviewed files. Closing a feedback-to-change loop would require the prior design state, the decision, and a responsible person or owner.

  2. Implementation gate

    Show the artifact and test

    A traceable implementation artifact and a test/result would be needed to show that a decision entered the project rather than remaining a discussion point.

  3. Reflexivity gate

    Return to the question

    Follow-up with the participant would be needed to examine whether the implemented response addressed the original concern. No such completed follow-up is documented.

Questionnaire status

Internal progress materials dated 25-26 May and 9 July report 344 collected responses. The correct instrument and raw dataset were not located or audited, the team acknowledged concentrated sampling, and the 7 July record says analysis remained pending. No questionnaire findings or participant conclusions can be drawn from the count.

A research agenda, not a conclusion

Interview preparation identifies the remaining agenda: field burden and the existing diagnostic workflow; sampling representativeness; false positives; discrimination between harmful and free-living nematodes; the relevant decision makers; responsible control after a result; and regulatory and implementation questions. These are planned lines of inquiry, not completed consultations or validated requirements.

Education is a separate stream of documented activity. It is not stakeholder consultation or product validation, and it cannot close an input-to-design loop.

Names and direct quotations are withheld because the reviewed files do not document public-attribution consent. This describes missing documentation only; the status is simply unverified.

Current documented status

The evidence supports documented consultation, an unanalysed questionnaire stream, and unresolved questions. No verified integrated Human Practices cycle is documented yet.
