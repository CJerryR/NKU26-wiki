A second internal note records an interview with an alumnus who had iGEM experience. Implementation questions

No freeze-dried format, partnership model, or sponsorship strategy is documented as implemented because of this advice.

What this challenged

Together, the records expose open questions about analytical feasibility, specificity, actual decision value, experimental sequencing, evidence quality, controlled use versus field use, and implementation constraints. They guide what must be investigated; they do not establish that stakeholder input changed the project.
