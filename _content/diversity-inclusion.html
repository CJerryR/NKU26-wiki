01 - Team

No unsupported inclusion story

The materials reviewed for this drafting pass do not document team demographics, recruitment practices, accommodation requests, accessibility measures, decision-making distribution, or a team-climate evaluation. We therefore do not infer that the working environment was inclusive simply from the existence of a multidisciplinary team.

A future team record should define respectful conduct, accessible meeting and documentation practices, routes for raising concerns, allocation of visible and invisible work, and periodic review. Only actions with dates, owners, participation boundaries, and outcomes should be reported as completed initiatives.

02 - Reach

Access questions for education and technology

Physical and digital access

The documented teaching decks show prepared material, not an accessibility audit. Future materials should be checked for readable contrast, text alternatives, navigable structure, plain-language summaries, and formats that do not assume a particular device or connection. Completion must be evidenced rather than presumed.

Language and context

The current evidence does not establish multilingual delivery or testing with different literacy levels. Translation alone would not be enough: terminology, examples, pace, and opportunities to ask questions should be reviewed with intended audiences before claiming that material is accessible.

Equity in a future tool

No user workflow, price, equipment burden, maintenance requirement, or distribution model has been validated. Inclusion therefore means asking who can collect a representative sample, operate the assay, interpret uncertainty, and act on a result, as well as who bears cost, risk, and waste.

03 - Reflection

Evaluation and consent come first

Current documented status

No participant demographic breakdown, accessibility evaluation, accommodation record, or public-image authorization was verified in the reviewed activity evidence. Future inclusion work should collect only necessary information, explain how it will be used, protect privacy, and report who was not reached as carefully as who was.
