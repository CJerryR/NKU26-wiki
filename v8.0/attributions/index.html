  1. Conceptualization: Designed and refined the molecular docking workflow, including strategies for receptor comparison and exploratory mutation analysis. Took primary responsibility for the overall design of the molecular dynamics studies.
  2. Investigation: Independently carried out the early molecular docking studies, including receptor comparisons and exploratory mutation analysis. Generated visualizations of molecular dynamics post-analysis results, including root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) profiles and residue-level contact statistics, to illustrate receptor and ligand motion and the temporal behavior of local interactions.
  6. Writing: Documented the docking and molecular dynamics workflows, results, and their interpretation. Wrote and revised the content of the Model page for the team Wiki.
