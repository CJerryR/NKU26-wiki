## Biological materials {#biological-materials toc="Biological materials"}

<p>This section documents the biological materials and experimental methods used in wet-lab work. <strong>Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA)</strong> and <strong>ribonucleic acid</strong> are the nucleic acids represented in the sequence entries. We summarize the designed <strong>single-guide ribonucleic acid</strong> sequences, constructed plasmids and engineered strains generated in our project. <strong>Polymerase chain reaction (PCR)</strong> underpins the cloning workflows described below. The chassis species is <em>Saccharomyces cerevisiae</em>; <strong><em>Escherichia coli</em> (<em>E. coli</em>)</strong> is used for plasmid propagation.</p>
