## What this draft actually offers {#what toc="The contribution"}

Our contribution at this stage is documentation, not a finished product. It is an honest, evidence-led package that a future team could pick up and verify: a chronological audit of the 18 supplied records, the practical protocol observations that go with it, the explicit negative and inconclusive outcomes, and a clearly drawn line between preliminary PCR evidence and sequence-confirmed editing.

To be clear about what this is *not*: we are not claiming a completed Registry contribution, a published dataset, a validated software or hardware tool, community adoption, a fully validated protocol set, or a working sensor. Those claims would require evidence the current records do not contain.

### Practical protocol observations {idx="02" href="/pages/protocols.html"}

These pages are working documentation, not final validated resources. They are most useful as a starting point to reproduce, correct, and extend — not as endpoints to cite.

## What would make this a real contribution {#boundary toc="Draft vs. final"}

The gap between this draft and a contribution we could publish is concrete and worth naming explicitly.

| Already documented (draft) | Required before a final contribution claim |
| --- | --- |
| Chronological notebook with result status | Confirmed sequences and Registry identifiers for each element |
| Recorded bench workflows and handling notes | Complete, reproducible protocols with programs and success criteria |
| Explicit negative and inconclusive outcomes | Complete source data behind the gels: some 0527/0530 records embed gel images, but uncropped and labelled images, expected sizes, and written interpretation are still missing |
| Preliminary 0607 ΔGal80 PCR conclusion | Independent validation and replication, including sequencing |
| Element and construct names with documented work | Clear licensing and versioning for reuse |

## Where we draw the line {#scope toc="Scope & honesty"}

We are not presenting the underlying scientific idea — a yeast GPCR reading a nematode ascaroside signal — as an iGEM contribution. A hypothesis is not a reusable artifact, and the records do not yet contain a concrete, verified deliverable built on it. What we can offer honestly is the documentation package above, which is why this page remains hidden until its contents are verified.

:::note[Why this page is still a draft]

A contribution claim is only as good as the evidence behind it. Until sequences, complete protocols, source data, and independent replication are in place, this stays a draft package to build on rather than a finished contribution to rely on.

:::
