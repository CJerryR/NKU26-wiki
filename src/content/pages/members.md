## Broad workstreams, not a roster {#subteams toc="Sub-teams"}

The reviewed records organize work into three broad workstreams: Wet Lab, Dry Lab, and Human Practices. These workstreams do not establish person-by-person assignments or membership.

:::cards{cols=3}

### Wet Lab {icon="flask"}

Reviewed Wet Lab evidence consists of documented experimental records and attempted workflows. It does not show an end-to-end detector.

### Dry Lab {icon="chip"}

Dry Lab evidence supports literature research and conceptual computational preparation. No validated model, software, or hardware system is documented.

### Human Practices {icon="people"}

Human Practices records include documented education materials and consultation records. They do not establish a completed integrated cycle.

:::
