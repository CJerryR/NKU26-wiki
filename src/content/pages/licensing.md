## Reusable under CC BY 4.0 {#license toc="Wiki license"}

Unless an item is credited otherwise beside the item or below, team-authored text, figures, photographs, and visual material on this wiki are released under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The complete legal text is stored in the repository root as `LICENSE`.

Reuse is permitted with appropriate credit, a link to the license, and an indication of whether changes were made. Trademarks, personal data, quoted statements, and third-party material remain subject to their own rights and permissions.

- The NKU-iGEM26 logo and detective mascot files were supplied as the team's visual identity for this wiki and are released with the team-authored wiki material under CC BY 4.0.
- Scientific literature, Registry entries, and other external sources are cited at the point where their information is used. Outbound links are references only; no external page supplies runtime assets to this wiki.

## What AI assisted with, and what it did not do {#ai toc="Responsible AI use"}

**Models used:** The existing project disclosure records OpenAI Codex, GPT-5 family, for repository inspection, static-build implementation, navigation and accessibility review, structural editing, and language polishing. For the 10–11 October 2026 GitLab preparation, OpenAI Codex (GPT-6) checked the official requirements, inspected the repository, prepared asset uploads, and configured the stage build and publication checks. The repository also records a prior Claude-assisted interaction-code integration in `docs/性能与滚动合并说明.md`; its exact model version is not recorded there and must be confirmed by the team. Historical model versions and the named human reviewers still require team verification.

**Human review:** Team members remain responsible for every published claim. AI-assisted text and code must be checked by a named team reviewer before the Wiki Freeze; scientific statements must be traced to the team's records or a real cited source, and code changes must pass the repository's build and audit commands.

**Prohibited uses:** AI has not been authorized to invent experiments, results, citations, quotations, consent, safety approvals, team contributions, or stakeholder feedback. It must not generate or alter evidentiary figures such as gels, microscopy, plots, or simulated experimental results presented as data. Decorative AI imagery, if added later, must identify the model next to the image and follow the licensing rules above.
