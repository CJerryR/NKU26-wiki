## AI use and human review {#ai-use-and-human-review toc="AI use and human review"}

Artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including OpenAI Codex, assisted Wiki development with code, debugging, code review, and translation of team-written text. The site footer credits Claude Opus 4.7, 4.8, 5.0, and 5.5, and OpenAI GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna for that work. Project tools, tasks, and review responsibilities are described in [Attributions → AI tool use](/attributions/#ai-tool-use).

## Decorative and evidentiary figures {#decorative-and-evidentiary-figures toc="Decorative and evidentiary figures"}

Inner-page opening illustrations were generated with OpenAI gpt-image-2.5 using team-provided artwork and layouts as references. They are released under CC BY 4.0 and are decorative, not experimental images or model results. Evidentiary figures present project observations or analyses. Reused material remains subject to its source license; figure captions identify direct AI-generated visual assets when applicable.
