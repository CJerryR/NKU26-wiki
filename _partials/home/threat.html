The hidden threat

  1. 1Searching for a host

    J2 juveniles move through the soil toward host roots.

  2. 2Releasing ascarosides

    Nematodes release small molecules called ascarosides. We follow two: ascr#3 and ascr#18.

  3. 3Invading the roots

    The nematode enters the root and sets up a feeding site. The root loses its normal structure.

  4. 4Symptoms above ground

    Damaged roots take up less water and nutrients. By the time leaves show symptoms, infection may be well under way.
