Nematode damage in China

Soybean cyst nematode alone costs China more than US$120 million in soybean yield a year.

  • Soybean cyst nematode Heterodera glycines, reported in 22 provinces. Infested fields typically lose 20–30% of yield, severe ones 60–70%.
  • Southern root-knot nematode Meloidogyne incognita, recorded in 20 provinces.
  • Xinjiang: root-knot nematodes in 57 of 130 vegetable samples, 2021–23 survey.
  • Main soybean regions: Northeast and Huang-Huai-Hai
Sources and how to read this map
