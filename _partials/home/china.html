Distribution and Impact of Soybean Cyst Nematode in China

Heterodera glycines, reported in 22 provincial-level regions

It is estimated that soybean cyst nematode causes more than US$120 million in soybean yield losses in China every year.

  • 20–30%typical yield loss in infested soybean fields
  • 60–70%in severely infested fields; extreme cases approach total loss

Soybean is the main host. Mung bean, pea, common bean and other food legumes can also be infected.

  • Province with published reports
  • Major soybean regions: Northeast and Huang-Huai-Hai

A report does not mean the whole province is affected. National losses are not divided among provinces.

Reported Distribution and Major Hosts of Southern Root-knot Nematode in China

Meloidogyne incognita, recorded in 20 provinces, plus detections in Xinjiang

Southern root-knot nematode has an extremely broad host range. It can infect vegetables, field crops, cash crops and many medicinal plants: more than 3,000 plant species in all.

  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Pepper
  • Eggplant
Hunan vegetable survey

About 80% disease incidence in greenhouse vegetables against about 10% in open fields; M. incognita made up 87% of the root-knot populations identified. These are incidence and species shares, not yield losses.

  • Province record (CABI)
  • Detected in protected vegetables, 2021–2023 survey

Records show where the species has been reported, not that whole provinces are heavily affected. Rice root-knot damage in China mostly involves another species, M. graminicola, which is kept separate.
