Here, color is a first piece of risk information to act on.

  1. Soil sampling

    Collect representative soil at the right time and place.

  2. Risk pre-screening

    Sense nematode signals on site for an early risk prompt.

  3. Professional confirmation

    Higher-risk or uncertain samples go to professional testing.

  4. Management decision

    Results, crop and region shape a targeted plan.

We hope to turn invisible chemical clues into a risk signal people can see and act on before planting.

Color is a prompt, not a verdict.
