Screening of reactivation-medium components for betaxanthin color development in engineered S. cerevisiae

Fourteen treatments and one blank control assayed across four 24-well plates. Color index: blue-channel gray level (ΔB).
Color gain relative to the blank control
Figure 6. Effect of reactivation-medium components on color gain. Color gain is expressed as ΔB, the difference between the mean blue-channel gray level of the blank control and that of each treatment (ΔB = Bblank − Bsample, arbitrary units on a 0–255 scale). Positive values indicate a more yellow well. Symbols are treatment means; horizontal bars span ±1 SD across replicate wells (n = 3, except n = 2 for the three glucose treatments and n = 6 for the blank).
The dashed vertical line marks ΔB = 0, the position of the blank control. It is a relative reference, not an absolute zero: it corresponds to a mean blue-channel gray level of 144.46, as labeled on the line, because the blue channel darkens as the well becomes more yellow. The dotted line marks the detection limit against the blank (ΔB = 7), approximately twice the standard error of the difference between a triplicate mean and the blank mean. Rows are ordered by ΔB and identified by compound class on the left margin.
The column headed mean ± SD reports the mean and standard deviation of each treatment, with the mean colored by magnitude (ochre above the blank, blue below, gray near zero). The final column gives the same difference as a percentage of the blank mean.
All wells contained SC medium with amino-acid-free YNB and 2.0% glucose as the reference carbon source, in a working volume of 1.0 mL. Glucose treatments varied the carbon source alone (1.0, 3.0 and 5.0%). Tween 80 treatments combined 0.1–1.5% surfactant with 0.2 g/L tyrosine, except one combination at 0.3 g/L. Images were acquired under fixed exposure and white balance and analyzed in ImageJ with a circular region of interest covering the central 55–60% of each well; the median blue-channel value of the region was used. Plate-to-plate illumination differences were normalized against the plate background, and residual edge-well shading was corrected using a set of 12 replicate wells not shown here. Abbreviations: Tyr, L-tyrosine; VC, ascorbic acid; Tw80, Tween 80.
