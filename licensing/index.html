01 — License

Reusable under CC BY 4.0

Unless an item is credited otherwise beside the item or below, team-authored text, figures, photographs, and visual material on this wiki are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. The complete legal text is stored in the repository root as LICENSE.

Reuse is permitted with appropriate credit, a link to the license, and an indication of whether changes were made. Trademarks, personal data, quoted statements, and third-party material remain subject to their own rights and permissions.

  • Scientific literature, Registry entries, and other external sources are cited at the point where their information is used. Outbound links are references only; no external page supplies runtime assets to this wiki.

03 — AI disclosure

What AI assisted with, and what it did not do

Model used: OpenAI Codex, GPT-5 family. It was used for repository inspection, implementation of the static build and compliance checks, navigation and accessibility review, structural editing, and language polishing.

Human review: Team members remain responsible for every published claim. AI-assisted text and code must be checked by a named team reviewer before the Wiki Freeze; scientific statements must be traced to the team's records or a real cited source, and code changes must pass the repository's build and audit commands.

Prohibited uses: AI has not been authorized to invent experiments, results, citations, quotations, consent, safety approvals, team contributions, or stakeholder feedback. It must not generate or alter evidentiary figures such as gels, microscopy, plots, or simulated experimental results presented as data. Decorative AI imagery, if added later, must identify the model next to the image and follow the licensing rules above.

Attributions Form

The official 2026 Attributions Form must also record AI-tool use and the people responsible for reviewing it. This page is a public disclosure, not a replacement for that required form.

04 — Review

Checks before publication

  1. Resolve every citation to a real DOI, Registry identifier, ISBN, or stable URL and confirm that it supports the adjacent claim.
  2. Confirm provenance, permission, and license for every image, icon, font, quotation, interview excerpt, and photograph.
  3. Verify that plots and scientific images correspond to archived raw data or documented computations and have not been AI-generated or cosmetically altered.
  4. Run the build, compliance audit, internal-link audit, and iGEM External Content Check before the Wiki Freeze.
